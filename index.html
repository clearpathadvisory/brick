BRICK — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 July 2026

BRICK is a puzzle game published by ClearPath Advisory. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and what you can do about it. It is written to be read, not to be survived.

The short version We do not ask for your name, email, or any account. Your game progress stays on your device. There is no advertising of any kind. One third party — our purchase provider — receives a receipt so that purchases can be restored.

What stays on your device

Your level progress, stars, high scores, and settings are stored locally on your phone. We do not upload them, we cannot see them, and deleting the app deletes them.

What third parties receive

RevenueCat — processes in-app purchases and remembers what you have bought so you can restore it on a new device. RevenueCat receives an anonymous purchase identifier and your receipt from Apple or Google. It does not receive your payment details; those are handled entirely by Apple or Google and are never visible to us. See RevenueCat's policy.

Advertising

BRICK contains no advertising. No banners, no video, no sponsored content, and no advertising SDK of any kind is included in the app. Nothing about your device is sent to an advertising network, because there isn't one.

Children

BRICK is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will remove it.

Your rights

Because we hold no account and no personal profile, there is generally nothing for us to export or delete on request. If you want the purchase record held by RevenueCat removed, contact us and we will pass the request on. If you are in the EU or UK, you may also complain to your data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top changes and the new version appears here before the change takes effect in the app.
