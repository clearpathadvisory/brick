BRICK — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 July 2026

BRICK is a puzzle game published by ClearPath Advisory. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and what you can do about it. It is written to be read, not to be survived.

The short version We do not ask for your name, email, or any account. Your game progress stays on your device. Two third parties — an advertising provider and a purchase provider — receive limited technical data so that ads and payments work.

What stays on your device

Your level progress, stars, high scores, and settings are stored locally on your phone. We do not upload them, we cannot see them, and deleting the app deletes them.

What third parties receive

Google AdMob — shows the optional rewarded video ads. If you choose to watch one, AdMob may receive your device advertising identifier, approximate location derived from your IP address, device model, and operating system version, in order to select and measure the ad. See Google's policy.

RevenueCat — processes in-app purchases and remembers what you have bought so you can restore it on a new device. RevenueCat receives an anonymous purchase identifier and your receipt from Apple or Google. It does not receive your payment details; those are handled entirely by Apple or Google and are never visible to us. See RevenueCat's policy.

Advertising, and how to limit it

BRICK shows no banners and no forced ads. The only advertising is an optional rewarded video that appears when you have failed a level, and only if you tap to watch it. Buying the full game removes even that offer permanently.

On iOS you will be asked whether the app may track you across other companies' apps. Declining is completely fine — the game is identical either way, and you can change your answer any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads.

Children

BRICK is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will remove it.

Your rights

Because we hold no account and no personal profile, there is generally nothing for us to export or delete on request. If you want the data held by AdMob or RevenueCat removed, contact us and we will pass the request to them. If you are in the EU or UK, you may also complain to your data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top changes and the new version appears here before the change takes effect in the app.
